# Seeds, Starters & Cultures — `bio`

The living stock everything grown, brewed or raised starts from: a gene vault sample revived into seed, cuttings,
spawn, yeasts, starter cultures and breeding stock, with the lab media they are kept on. Seed goes to `grn`, `veg` and
`frt`, which pass grain and produce back to have seed saved from it; yeasts and cultures go to `ing`, `bev`, `brw` and
`prs`; algae, insect, fish, quail and cell stock to `prt`; inoculants, worms and biocontrol to `soi` and `san`;
houseplant cuttings and aquarium fish to `grd`. Living stock dies if it sits; seed and dried cultures keep.
