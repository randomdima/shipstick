# Personal Hygiene — `hyg`

Keeping the crew member clean: soap and the lye, glycerin, surfactant and scent it is made from; shampoo, toothpaste,
deodorant, wipes and the rest of the washbasin's consumables; razors, toothbrushes, combs, clippers, sponges and towels
— tools a wash or a groom needs, worn in use, the towels and cloths into dirty laundry; and the shower. Most washing
leaves `san-001` Grey Water; wipes, worn brushes and dull blades leave spent forms for `san` and `scr`; menstrual
products leave `toi-045`.
