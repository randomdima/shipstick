# Stress Relief — `rlx`

Things that take stress down. Lavender, chamomile and rosemary from the capsule's pots are dried and stilled into
essential oils; oils, salts, wax and cloth become sachets, candles, massage oils and bath soaks; foam, beads, springs
and steel combs become mats, stress balls and music boxes. What sits in the cabin — sachets, candles, lamps, a window
cushion, a locket worn — lowers stress by the hour; what the crew member squeezes, strikes, winds, lies on or massages
with is a tool, and gives its calm only in the minutes a way spends with it (ECO-11). Tools wear out to scrap in use;
sachets fade, candles burn down to stubs that are remelted, and oils go rancid. Soaks, massages and meditation are run
at the tub, the table and the nook.
