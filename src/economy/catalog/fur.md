# Furniture & Device Kits — `fur`

Where new fixtures and the cabin's furniture come from. Boards, rails, joints, hinges, foam and cloth are cut, bent and
sewn into device frames and panels; those are packed into kits that drop on the floor and become the universal fixtures
or this subsection's own shops, or built up into chairs, cabinets, curtains and lamp stands. The furniture holds rest,
stress and `cabin.clean` while it stands; a desk, a hobby table and a ladder help only while the crew member writes,
builds or climbs at them, so they are tools a way needs (ECO-11). All of it wears into scuffed boards, worn upholstery
and flat cushions that go back to the bench or to salvage. Device frames and fasteners go to every subsection that
builds a kit.
