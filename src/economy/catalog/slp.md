# Sleep Aids — `slp`

What helps a crew member fall asleep and stay asleep, beyond the bedding. `veg`'s dried chamomile, lavender and hops are
blended into tea sachets, extracts and balms; melatonin (fermented from yeast) and the herb extracts are pressed with
`med`'s binders and shells into tablets, capsules, gummies, drops and syrup; earplugs and mouth guards are moulded from
silicone and foam; masks and sleepwear are sewn from `fib` cloth; sound boxes and sunrise lamps are assembled from
electronics. The one-shot aids are taken at the mess table for `rest` and less `stress` (the stronger ones at a little
`toxin`); the durables lie on the cot and work an hour at a time. Sleepwear and masks soil into `cln-003` Dirty Laundry
and come back from the wash; earplugs and guards wear into plastic scrap, sound boxes into e-waste.
