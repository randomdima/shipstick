# Galley Stores — `ing`

The galley's pantry. Oilseeds are pressed into oils, brine is boiled down to salt, sugar beets to sugar, dried crops milled
into spices; stocks and sauces simmer in the kettle; soy cream is churned into butter and curdled into cheese;
soy, rice and fruit ferment into soy sauce, miso and vinegar. Almost everything here is an input to meals, preserves and
snacks; a few cheeses and spreads are eaten as they come. Press cake, bittern, beet pulp, lees and used frying oil go on to
feed, fuel and chemistry.
