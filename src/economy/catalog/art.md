# Arts, Crafts & Music — `art`

What the crew member makes with their hands. Pigment is milled and bound into paints; paper, canvas, clay, yarn,
thread, beads and cord are the supplies; the easel, the craft desk, the knitting frame and the kiln use them up into
finished pieces (a painting, a figurine, a knitted scarf) that ease stress from the wall, the shelf or the body, or go
through a door wrapped as a gift. Instruments are built from shells, strings and reeds; an instrument, a songbook, sheet
music and a filled sketchbook are tools (ECO-11), giving only while a way plays or reads them, and wear with that play:
strings go slack, heads tear, and the instrument is restrung or re-headed.
