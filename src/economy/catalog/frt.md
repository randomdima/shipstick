# Fruit & Berries — `frt`

Fruit raw to final: berries, dwarf orchard and citrus trees, vines and melons, bananas, cacao and coffee, grown from
`bio`'s cuttings, grafts and seed in `soi`'s media, then eaten fresh, cut, pressed or dried. Fresh fruit is a little
satiety with some fun, hydration and immunity; cut fruit and dried fruit go to `snk`, `mel` and `prs`; musts and
pressed juice to `bev` and `brw`; cacao beans to `snk` and green coffee to `bev`. Peel, rinds, cores, pits, pomace,
husks and prunings go to `soi`'s compost and `prt`'s feed. Berries spoil in days and overripen first; citrus and apples
keep for weeks; dried fruit keeps.
