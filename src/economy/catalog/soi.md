# Growth Media & Nutrients — `soi`

Everything a root grows in or feeds on, raw to final: crop residue, food scraps and urine made into compost and
fertilizer, mineral salts mixed into hydroponic solution, substrates fired in the kiln, potting blends, and the sprays,
traps and containers of plant care. Its goods go to the farms of `grn`, `veg` and `frt`, to `bio`'s propagation, to
`prt`'s algae and to `grd`'s houseplants and aquaria; its spent media come back to be steamed or composted.
