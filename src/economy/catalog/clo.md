# Garments & Wearables — `clo`

What the crew member wears from the locker. Cloth, knit, felt and fleece from `fib` are cut to pattern into panels and
sewn with notions (buttons, snaps, zips, binding) into jumpsuits, thermal layers, jumpers, gloves and caps; socks and
liners come off the hosiery knitter; boots, trainers and slippers go through the last press with soles and insoles.
Kept in the locker, clothing gives `cabin.heat`, `hygiene`, less `stress` or some `fun` an hour at a time until it is
worn into `cln-003` Dirty Laundry; the wash returns `cln-004`, which is sorted back into garments or the laundered
clothes set, or cut into rags once worn through. Footwear wears into rubber scrap or broken parts.
