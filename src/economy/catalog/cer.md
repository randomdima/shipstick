# Ceramics, Glass & Composites — `cer`

Glass, ceramic, silicon and fibre composites, the foundry's non-metal line. Sand and minerals from the mine (`ore`) are
melted into glass batch, then panes, tubes, vials and optical blanks; clay slip is fired into tiles, bricks,
insulators and pipe; sand and carbon are pulled into polysilicon, silicon boules for the wafer fab (`ecp`), solar silicon and sapphire; glass, basalt and carbon are spun
into fibre, woven into cloth, laid up with resin (`pol`) and cured into the panels, beams and frames that hull plates
(`hul`), device kits and fixtures are built from. Regolith and slag (`met`) are fired into bricks and wool too. Slip
dries, prepreg cures on the shelf, and every line leaves cullet, shards or offcuts that are remelted, ground into grog
or sent to recycling (`scr`).
