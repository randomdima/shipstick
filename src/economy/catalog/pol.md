# Polymers, Seals & Lubricants — `pol`

Plastics, rubber, seals, sealants, adhesives and lubricants. Pellets and resins are made from volatiles and chemicals,
and again from plastic scrap, moulding sprue and rubber offcuts; they are extruded into film, sheet, tubing and filament,
moulded into housings and bushings, cured into rubber and silicone, and foamed. Base oils are refined into oils and
greases, and used oil is refined again. Pellets and film go to packaging, seals and oils to mechanical parts and every
machine, filament to the workbench, sealants and putty to a leaking cabin.
