# Books & Media — `lib`

What the crew member reads, watches, plays and listens to. Paper is printed into page stacks, sewn and bound into
books; blank media cards and cartridges are flashed from the library archive with films, music, audiobooks and games;
screens, boards and shells are built into readers, players and consoles. Books and media are tools (ECO-11): a book is
read, a film card watched on the viewer tablet or the film screen, a music chip or audio card played on the music player,
a cartridge played on the console, and the fun or calm comes from the crew member's time spent so. They wear only while
in use: books dog-eared or into loose pages for rebinding, data media into corrupted media cards and dead cartridges to
wipe and reflash, and readers into e-waste. Printed issues are read once and left as waste paper.
