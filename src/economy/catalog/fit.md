# Exercise — `fit`

What keeps bone and muscle in low gravity. Latex, tubing, foam, cast weights, webbing and cord are made into bands,
mats, weights and straps — tools a workout needs, which give fitness only while the crew member spends the minutes,
and wear in use into snapped bands, rubber crumb or scrap; routine cards are worked through once for fitness, a lower
stress or a better rest, at the cost of some hygiene. The treadmill, the rowing machine, the cycle ergometer and the
resistance station are where the real workouts run; their belts, cords, pads and cables wear out and are made again at
the gear bench and the foam mould.
