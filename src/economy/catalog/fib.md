# Fibres & Textiles — `fib`

Everything woven, knitted or felted that the quarters sew from. Cereal straw and crop stalks are retted into plant and
bast fibre, corn husks and `scr-005` Fabric Scraps are carded, and the polymer line draws nylon, polyester, aramid and
stretch fibre; fibre is spun into yarn, thread, twine and rope, dyed, and woven, knitted or felted into cloth, knits,
felt, webbing and padding. Nothing here is worn or slept on: its goods go to bedding (`bed`), sleep aids (`slp`),
clothing (`clo`) and insulation (`thm`), and what they wear out comes back as scraps to card again.
