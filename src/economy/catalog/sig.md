# Notes, Tokens & Signals — `sig`

Capsules never speak, so everything one says to another goes through a door as an object. Waste paper is pulped and
pressed into note paper, card stock and gift wrap; ink, pencils and wax write and seal it into notes, letters and
cards; blank tags and tokens are stamped into requests ("need water"), answers and thanks; and finished goods of the
other recreation subsections are wrapped as gifts. Whoever receives one gets less stress or some fun, and keeps the
paper, the wrap, or a blank tag or token to send back. The ledger, the logbook, the door boards, the codebook, the
pictogram dictionary, the blinker lamp and the flags are tools (ECO-11): they ease or amuse only for the minutes spent
writing, reading or signalling with them. A medal, a bead string or a keepsake box works by being kept.
