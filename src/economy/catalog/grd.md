# Ornamental Plants & Animals — `grd`

Living things that calm the crew and freshen the air: houseplants, potted herbs, bonsai, terrariums, aquarium fish and
small insect companions. Cuttings, seedlings and breeding stock from `bio`, potting media and pots from `soi`, and feed
become plants and creatures that lower stress and nudge the cabin's oxygen and carbon dioxide while they sit on the
shelf or live in the tank. Left alone they decay: houseplants wilt, herbs and bonsai overgrow, terrariums parch,
animals go hungry; tended in time they recover — and the trimmings feed the medicine and calm subsections — or die into
dead plants and animals that go to compost.
