# Mechanical Parts & Tools — `mec`

Fasteners, gears, bearings, springs, shafts, valves, pumps, fans and motors, turned, milled, stamped and wound from
metal stock and assembled with seals and oil. Most go out as the spare parts that machines across the ship wear
through: a fitted service kit restores a device and leaves the worn part behind, which is rebuilt here or scrapped.
Hand tools are forged and ground here too; they keep the workbench and the machines going until they wear out into
scrap.
