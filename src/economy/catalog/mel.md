# Cooked Meals — `mel`

The galley's hot food: staples, proteins and pantry goods cooked into porridges, breads, soups, stews, curries,
stir-fries, bakes, sandwiches, dumplings and feasts, and served on a clean dish at the mess table. Meals give the most
satiety; comfort food and feasts also ease stress and raise fun. A plated meal leaves a dirty dish and often food
scraps. Hot food spoils within a day or two, most of it first into cold leftovers; bread goes stale.
