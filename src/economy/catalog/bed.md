# Beds & Bedding — `bed`

Everything that makes a cot worth lying on. Fills (rag, fibre, wool, grain hull, glass bead) and foam slabs are stuffed
and cut; ticking, sheet cloth and quilted panels are sewn from `fib` cloth; together they make pillows, blankets,
duvets, sheets, mattresses and sleeping bags, and the cot's own spare parts. Bedding lies on the cot and gives `rest`
(and, the comfortable kinds, less `stress`) an hour at a time. Sheets, covers and bags soil into `cln-003` Dirty Laundry
and come back from the wash as `cln-004`, which the steamer presses into bedding again; pillows go flat, mattresses wear
and duvets go threadbare, and those are restuffed, patched or unpicked into fill and `scr-005` Fabric Scraps.
