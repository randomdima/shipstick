# Rations & Preserves — `prs`

Food that keeps, for the long haul and for passing through doors. Crops, proteins and cooked meals are canned into
tins, pickled and jammed into jars, dehydrated, freeze-dried, frozen, vacuum-sealed and pressed into bars, and packed
into pouches, ration packs and crates. Each gives somewhat less than a fresh meal but lasts weeks or never spoils.
Pouched food leaves an empty pouch, a tin an empty tin can, a jar an empty jar, all for the stores to wash
and fill again. Frozen goods slowly
freezer-burn; old emergency packs expire.
