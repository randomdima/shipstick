# Vegetables & Greens — `veg`

Fresh produce, raw to chopped: sprouts and microgreens, leafy greens, fruiting vegetables, roots and alliums, herbs,
tea, hops and mushrooms, grown from `bio`'s seed and spawn in `soi`'s media, then washed, trimmed, chopped or dried.
Greens and chopped mixes go to `mel` and `prs`; onions, garlic, chilies and herbs to `ing`; tea, chamomile and mint to
`bev` and `slp`; lavender and aloe to `rlx`, `hyg` and `med`; hops to `brw`; roots, stalks, leaves and skins go to
`soi`'s compost and `prt`'s feed. Leaves wilt in days, roots keep for weeks, and dried forms keep.
