# Grains & Staples — `grn`

The crew's bulk calories, raw to dough: wheat, barley, oats, rice, corn, legumes, sunflowers and tubers grown from
`bio`'s seed in `soi`'s media, then threshed, hulled and milled into grain, flour, meal and starch, and kneaded into
dough and pasta. Grain, flour and dough go to `ing`, `mel`, `snk`, `prs` and `brw`, and grain back to `bio` for seed;
bran, chaff and germ go to `prt`'s feed; straw, hulls, cobs, haulm and peel go to `soi`'s compost and mulch. Grain and
flour keep; sheaves, tubers and dough do not.
