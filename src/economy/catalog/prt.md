# Proteins — `prt`

The farm's and bio lab's protein. Crickets, mealworms and fly larvae are raised on food scraps; quail lay eggs; algae,
mycelium and cultured meat grow in vats; soybeans are pressed into tofu and fermented into tempeh and natto; tilapia and
shrimp grow in the aquaponic tank. Raw harvests are cleaned, milled, minced and formed into the flours, fillets, patties
and mince the galley cooks into meals, rations and snacks. Frass, litter, sludge, shells and spent media go back to the
soil and the waste works.
