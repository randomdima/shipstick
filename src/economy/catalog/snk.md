# Snacks & Confectionery — `snk`

The galley's sweets and nibbles: dried cacao roasted and refined into chocolate, sugar boiled into sweets and caramels,
biscuits, cakes and pastries baked, crisps and crackers fried, corn popped, ice cream churned. Each gives a little
satiety and some fun or a little less stress; a cake or a party platter is a celebration, and a box of chocolates or a
jar of sweets is the gift most often passed through a door. Crisps and biscuits go stale, chocolate blooms, ice cream
melts; crumbs and melted cream feed later treats.
