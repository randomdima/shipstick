# Games & Pastimes — `fun`

Cards, board games, puzzles, dice, toys and model kits. Card stock and ink are printed into card sheets, boards and
puzzle pages; plastic is moulded into dice, tokens, tiles and model sprues; the pieces are boxed into game sets and kits.
A game set or a toy is a tool (ECO-11): a way at the table or in the crew member's hands needs it, takes their minutes
and gives the fun, and play wears it into loose pieces (reground for the mould), worn cards or waste paper. A puzzle
book is used up; a model kit is built at the bench into a model that keeps fun up from the shelf. Finished games go
through doors as gifts.
