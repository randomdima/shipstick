# Containers & Packaging — `pak`

The vessels every other subsection fills: pouches, bottles, canisters, tins, jars, tubes, cartons, sachets, vials,
drums, crates and sacks, with their caps, lids, seals and labels. Each is made here from film, preforms and blanks,
filled elsewhere, and comes back empty when its contents are used: an empty is washed into a clean one and filled
again, or, once cracked, dented or single-use, crushed and shredded into scrap. Wraps, inserts, sachets and labels go
to stores and the galley; organizers keep a cabin tidy while they last.
