# Chemicals — `chm`

The chem lab's stock: acids, bases, salts, solvents and reagents. Sulfur, salt, brine, limestone and frosts from the
mine (`ore`) are reacted, electrolysed, distilled, calcined and crystallised into the base chemicals, and those into
monomers for plastics (`pol`), fertilizer salts for the farm (`soi`), lye, surfactant and glycerol for soap and
detergent (`hyg`, `cln`), scrubber and desiccant chemicals for the air (`fil`), iodine and chelators for medicine
(`med`, `rad`), electrolytes and cathode oxides for cells (`pwr`), flux and etchant for the foundry and electronics,
pigments and dyes for paint and cloth, and the nitric acid, ammonia and peroxide the drive's propellants (`prp`) start
from. Peroxide, bleach and quicklime
do not keep. Spent acid, solvent, catalyst, brine and still bottoms are real leftovers: neutralised, redistilled,
regenerated or sent to recycling (`scr`).
