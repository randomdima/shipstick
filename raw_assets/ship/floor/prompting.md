# Floor tiles — what the wording did

- **No bolts** (`deck_c`, score 4, in use): plate edges are "only a fine flush weld line along that edge, slightly
  darker, with no bevel, no rivets and no raised edge". The tiles darken toward their corners, which shows as a faint
  cross where four meet; the page crops the floor 12%. The threshold (`deck_threshold`, from `doors_c`) lies inside
  each door, its "broad black and yellow hazard-striped sill along its top edge where the door stands".

- **The deck is laid in plates** (`deck_b`, score 4, in use; the owner: "make pattern less repeatable. So it does not
  look like individual block. Same for floor"). Each tile is drawn for the plate edges it shows: "Where a plate ends,
  the tile shows that plate's edge: a narrow bevelled seam with a line of small rivets just inside it. Where the
  tile's edge lies inside a plate, that edge has no seam, no bevel, no rivets and no line at all" — inner, one edge,
  corner, two opposite, three, four, and three one-cell fittings, all as asked; the page lays plates of several sizes
  and turns each tile to its edges. "No single distinctive mark, stain or scratch that would show when a tile repeats"
  kept the plates even. A cut tile has a dark fringe that shows as a hairline inside a plate: the page draws each 4%
  larger than its cell. Everything below is the earlier one-plate-a-cell deck, unused.

From `deck_a-v1`, the devices' style picture as image 1, "seen straight down", "fills its own square edge to edge".

- **Every tile came out as one riveted plate with a bevelled edge**, not a seamless texture: laid side by side they
  read as a deck of plates, which is what a deck is. Kept so (score 4).
- **v2 (`cdeck_a`) is the lived-on, kept deck** (GAM-12): "light scuffs, worn paint, faint boot marks — no grime, no
  rust, no oil stains, no puddles, no litter"; the oil-stained tile became one "worn smooth along a walkway". Plates
  as before (score 4).
- The hazard-band tile (v2) has its band along the left edge only; lay it on a floor's edge column.
- On a transparent background each tile still filled its square, so the owner's settings held.
