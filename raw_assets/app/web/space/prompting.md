# Space — what the wording did

`space_a` (score 4, in use): nine patches of sky for the page round the ship, a plain `gen`.

- **"Transparent background everywhere there is no star" still drew each patch as an opaque near-black square**
  (alpha 253, about `#0a0b0b`), so the patches cannot be laid over one another plainly. The page lightens them
  together over `--space`, the same black, and no square's edge shows.
- **The sky as seen "through thick reinforced glass… muted and cold, no neon, no pink, no purple, no lens flares"**
  kept it dull and in style. The sparse patches came out fainter than asked (a few visible points of "some forty"),
  which suits a background.
- **A haze asked to drift "across the square, fading out well before every edge" ran off two corners** (v1). The
  `--mask` redo (v2) fixed both: "a small soft cloud… in the middle of its square, fading smoothly to nothing well
  inside the square, so a wide margin of plain dark space runs round all four edges and no haze touches or is cut by
  any edge".
