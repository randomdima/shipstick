# Props and trim — what the wording did

From `props_a-v1` (4×4) and `trim_a-v1` (3×3), the devices' style picture as image 1 with "drawn flat and face on".

- **State pairs came out matched** (button up and pressed, toggle off and on, one lamp unlit and lit three colours),
  so a state swap is one image for another of the same size.
- **"Its whole centre empty and fully transparent"** gave real holes in `bezel_screen` and `frame_panel`, usable as
  border images.
- **"Seamless on every side"** gave flat panels whose riveted edges show at each repeat; they read as plates, not as a
  seamless texture. Ask for "no rivets, no edge" when a texture must vanish under tiling.
- The gauge took tick marks without numbers, as asked.
